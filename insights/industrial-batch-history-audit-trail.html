ENGINEERING INSIGHT

Batch History and Audit Trail in Industrial SCADA

Production history and audit trail solve different problems. Batch history describes what the process produced; audit records explain who changed or commanded the system and when.

Keep production and audit records distinct

A batch record typically contains batch identity, recipe revision, targets, measured values, start/end times and outcome. Audit records focus on user actions such as parameter changes, recipe selection, acknowledgements and manual commands.

Timestamp at the correct layer

Events should be timestamped as close as practical to the authoritative action. Define ownership clearly so PLC events and SCADA actions do not create contradictory histories.

Recipe traceability

Store enough recipe identity to reconstruct what was actually executed. A recipe name alone is weak traceability when the same recipe can be edited over time; revision or immutable identifiers improve the record.

Design for investigation

History screens should support filtering by time, batch, recipe and event type. Export is useful, but the primary objective is rapid investigation of production deviations and operator actions.
