04 / PROCESS & MOTION

Molded Fiber Process Automation

Process automation synchronizing multi-axis servo motion, vacuum, tanks, pumps, valves and multi-zone temperature control in one production cycle.

PROJECT EXAMPLE
01 / NEED

Bring motion and fluid/vacuum process timing together

Mechanical axes were directly linked to tank levels, pumps, valves, spray timing, vacuum points and temperature zones.

02 / SOLUTION

Process parameters tied to axis motion

Position, torque and offset parameters were connected to production settings. Spray and vacuum points were managed centrally while tank feedback controlled pumps and valves.
