04 / PROCESS & MOTION

Molded Fiber Process Automation

Process automation synchronizing multi-axis servo motion, vacuum process, tank-pump-valve control and multi-zone temperature management within the same production cycle.

PROJECT EXAMPLE
01 / CONTEXT & CHALLENGE

Bring motion control and fluid/vacuum processing into the same timing model

Mechanical axis movements were directly linked to tank levels, pumps, valves, spray timing, vacuum points and temperature zones. Product quality required material application and vacuum activation at defined mechanical angles, while transfer axes had to move with the correct torque and position values.

02 / ENGINEERING APPROACH

Process parameters tied directly to axis motion

Position, torque and offset parameters for multiple servo axes were linked to production settings. Spray start/end angles and vacuum activation points were managed through a central parameter structure. Main, intermediate and overflow tank levels were monitored through transmitter feedback to control pumps and valves. Multi-point control was applied across separate temperature zones, while overall machine state was managed with a PackML-oriented approach and a TwinSAFE safety layer.

03 / ENGINEERING SCOPE

Main engineering areas covered in the project.

  • Multi-axis servo motion
  • Position, offset and torque parameters
  • Tank-level and pump control
  • Valve and flow sequences
  • Spray / vacuum timing logic
  • Multi-zone temperature control
  • PackML machine-state management
  • TwinSAFE and safety I/O integration
