01 / PROCESS & SCADA

Integrated Process Automation & SCADA

Multi-stage production control combining PLC, operator interface, recipe management and production traceability in one automation system.

PROJECT EXAMPLE
01 / NEED

Turn separate process stages into one production flow

Material preparation, precision dosing, mixing, extrusion and drying each had their own equipment and upstream/downstream dependencies. The goal was to manage the complete line as one controlled, traceable process.

02 / SOLUTION

PLC and SCADA designed around the same process model

Machine, subsystem and equipment levels were separated in the PLC using explicit state machines and process interlocks. SCADA combined live process views, recipes, production history, audit records, alarms and reporting.
