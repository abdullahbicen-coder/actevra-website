INDUSTRIAL DATA GATEWAY

Collecting machine data is not enough.
It must become operational evidence.

We unify data from different machine vendors in a common, timestamped and traceable data layer without unnecessarily replacing working PLCs.

What it delivers

From PLC tags to operational evidence.

Multi-protocol acquisition

Legacy and modern machines through ADS, OPC UA, Modbus TCP/RTU, PROFINET, EtherNet/IP and suitable industrial gateways.

Reliable recording

Timestamps, source and data quality, with store-and-forward behavior for connection interruptions.

Deliver production data to existing higher-level systems, historians, MQTT or reporting layers.

Root-cause investigation

Correlate alarms, downtime, micro-stops and process changes on a common timeline.
