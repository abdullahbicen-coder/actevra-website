ENGINEERING INSIGHT

Building a Maintainable TwinCAT 3 + WPF SCADA Architecture

Industrial SCADA becomes difficult to maintain when PLC communication, UI state, alarms and production data are tightly coupled.

Separate communication from presentation

Keep TwinCAT ADS access behind a connection and subscription layer. ViewModels consume typed HMI models instead of opening ADS handles directly.

Use typed PLC/HMI contracts

Mirror carefully designed PLC HMI structures in .NET and keep packing, field order and ownership explicit.

Modular SCADA

Prism modules separate alarms, recipes, production history, device faceplates and subsystem views.

Operational diagnostics matter

Expose PLC connection state, command ownership, raw drive state and communication health.
