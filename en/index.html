Modernization & Multi-Vendor Integration

Modernize the factory you already have.

We develop new systems with TwinCAT 3 and Siemens TIA Portal, and modernize existing installations through PLC/HMI revisions, drive upgrades and data integration. The scope follows your installed equipment and production requirements.

Platforms and modernization scope

Our primary engineering platforms for new projects and retrofit work include Beckhoff TwinCAT 3 and Siemens TIA Portal with SIMATIC S7-1200 and S7-1500. For installed machine bases, we can also integrate, acquire data from and progressively modernize systems using Allen-Bradley / Rockwell Automation, Schneider Electric / Modicon, Mitsubishi, Omron, ABB, B&R, Bosch Rexroth, Delta and FATEK PLC platforms where the required engineering tools and interfaces are available.

Our objective is not to replace a working controller simply because it comes from a different vendor. Legacy and modern machines can be improved progressively through PLC/HMI and drive retrofits, additional instrumentation, safety upgrades, data acquisition and higher-level system integration while maintaining production continuity.

Approach

Software designed for field diagnostics.

Explicit state machines.
The active state and the reason a sequence is waiting should be visible online.

Single command owner.
Clear ownership prevents uncontrolled commands from multiple software layers.

Diagnostics first.
Interlocks, communication, safety and device faults should be distinguishable.

Designed for operators.
SCADA should explain what is happening instead of merely displaying PLC variables.
